One Sentence

A board game about building stargate infrastructure that makes distant people, markets, or worlds newly connected.

Target Feel

  • Connection matters more than conquest.
  • Players benefit from their own network and from predicting what others need.
  • The board state should become more interesting as links accumulate.
  • Every new gate should create options, obligations, or tension.

Out Of Scope For Now

  • Lore-heavy campaign play.
  • Hidden movement.
  • Direct combat as the primary loop.
  • App-assisted rules.
