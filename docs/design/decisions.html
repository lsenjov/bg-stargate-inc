Date Decision Reason Revisit When
2026-07-22 Factory multipliers affect dollar running costs only. Players choose factory order; modules resolve oldest-first with immediate output reuse. Keeps inputs legible while making factory order and production chains meaningful choices. Production chains become automatic or later multipliers do not constrain decisions.
2026-07-22 Factories are permanent, empty slots are not factories, and mixed-owner exoplanet factories are operated entirely by the connected player. Gives placement lasting weight and makes shared exoplanet infrastructure usable without ownership exceptions. Shared factories create ownership disputes or permanent placement is too punishing.
2026-07-22 Players start with six held modules, their total home construction resources, and $20; modules may remain held for later connections. The first connection remains a real choice instead of a mandatory setup action. Players who delay home construction cannot recover or opening choices become scripted.
2026-06-27 Use simultaneous selection cards for connection attempts. Hidden, simultaneous choices create player-to-player reads around trades, exoplanets, self-production, and pause timing. Selection resolution creates too much failed-action frustration or pause dominates the round.
2026-06-27 Use plain HTML for human-facing docs. The repo is documentation-first and does not need a site framework yet. A public website, generated rulebook, or component database becomes useful.
