Glossary

Stable terms for rules and components.

Term Working Meaning Status
Stargate A connection point or infrastructure piece used to link distant locations. Draft
Connection A usable link between two points in the network. Draft
Route A path through one or more connections. Draft
Network The full set of available stargates and connections. Draft
Contract A scoring or reward condition tied to specific connections or deliveries. Candidate
Exoplanet A middle target players can connect to during a connection selection round. Draft
Pause A selection card that lets a player wait until non-pause choices are visible before choosing any final target card from hand. Draft
Played Card A face-up selection card that cannot be played again until returned to hand. Draft
Failed Connection A connection attempt that does not create a connection after pause choices resolve. Draft
Small Action Compensation given to a player who failed to connect. Unresolved
