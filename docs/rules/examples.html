Rules Examples

Worked examples for common and disputed situations.

Back to docs index

Example Format

  1. State the board position.
  2. State the active player's intent.
  3. Apply the exact rule.
  4. Record the result.
  5. Link any open question created by the example.

Needed Examples

  • Two players choose each other, connect, and trade.
  • One player chooses an exoplanet and connects to it.
  • One player chooses themself and produces internally.
  • Two players choose the same exoplanet and fail to connect.
  • A pause player connects to an exoplanet blocked by other players.
  • Two pause players choose the same exoplanet and fail to connect.
  • A pause player connects to a player who targeted them.
  • A pause player chooses a player who did not target them and fails to connect.
  • Two pause players choose each other and connect.
  • A player self-connects and returns all played cards to hand.
  • A factory with five modules runs the oldest three and stops, leaving the newest two unrun.
  • A player runs three factories at 1×, 2×, and 3× without increasing the earlier factories' costs.
  • A Solar Farm's Energy output pays the input of a later Farm during the same connection.
  • A player operates every module in a mixed-owner exoplanet factory, paying all costs and receiving all outputs.
  • A player spends multiple Teams on an exoplanet module draw, keeps one module, and discards the rest.
  • A player spends one Team to take one module from an exoplanet discard pile instead of drawing.
  • A player installs a module at an exoplanet by paying its construction cost plus one Team and marks ownership.
  • A failed player takes a small compensation action.
  • Resolving a blocked or contested route.
  • Scoring a contract.
  • Triggering the end game.
